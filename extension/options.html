Voice2Text

YOUR AUDIO. YOUR MAC.

Connect to your Mac

Voice2Text turns your browser's audio into text using a speech model on this computer. Add the connection key once to pair this browser.

Settings are stored in this browser only.

Start the local service from the Voice2Text folder, or register the browser launcher from the voice2text-run menu.

Copy the connection key without displaying it:

./voice2text-run copy-key

Then open a video, click the Voice2Text icon, and choose Start captions. Audio and captions stay on your Mac.
